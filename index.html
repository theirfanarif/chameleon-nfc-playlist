Playlist controls

Pause freezes the playlist timer, not the NFC hardware. Stop leaves the last identity loaded. Restore puts the original UID back.

Playback settings

With reset enabled, the app disables HF emulation during the gap, loads the next UID, then enables HF again. This may help the phone recognise a new tag. It has not been tested on your firmware 2.3.

Manual tag emulator

Choose a missed tag and present it again without restarting the playlist.

Manual emulation runs independently of the playlist. Stop Manual disables HF only if experimental reset is enabled; otherwise the UID remains loaded.
